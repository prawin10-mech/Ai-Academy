## a. Today's angle
Dev tools can disappear, so keep your agent setup in your own repo. Reported on Nov 4 to 5: Meta is acquiring Conductor, a tool that lets developers hand parts of a coding project to several AI agents. Munder Difflin says new sign-ups stopped on Nov 4, Conductor Cloud goes read-only on Nov 13 and data deletion starts Nov 20, so users should export. The Neuron, citing TechCrunch, only gives Nov 20. Why it matters to web developers: if your prompts, agent rules and task lists live only inside a vendor app, a sale or shutdown becomes your problem.
