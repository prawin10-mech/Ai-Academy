**Reusable reply draft: "web developer, where do I start with AI?"** (edit to match the real post):
"Start with something you can already ship. Build a small app where your backend calls a model API, saves the messages in your database and shows the answer in a simple UI. Next, add retrieval: put a few documents you know well into the app and print the extracted text before you split it, because many bugs start there. Then write 15 to 20 questions with the answers you expect and rerun them after every change. If you use an AI tool to scaffold the app, review the auth and database rules yourself. I built a free 26-week path that follows this order, so I am biased: https://ai-academy-mauve-five.vercel.app/ai-engineer-roadmap. Disclosure: I built this."

**Reusable reply draft: "how does RAG work?"**:
"Think of it as search plus a prompt. You split your documents into small chunks, store them so you can search by meaning, and when a question comes in you fetch the best few chunks and paste them into the prompt with the question. The model then answers from that text instead of from memory. Most failures come from bad chunks or bad parsing, not the model, so print what you retrieved for 10 questions before changing anything else. I built a free 26-week path with a RAG section, so I am biased: https://ai-academy-mauve-five.vercel.app/ai-engineer-roadmap. Disclosure: I built this."
