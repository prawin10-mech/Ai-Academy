## a. Today's angle
Banks are finding third-party AI agents inside customer accounts. SecurityWeek (Oct 23, citing Axios Oct 22) says the agents often sign in with the customer's own credentials, some seem to act on their own, and many banks cannot tell if an action was directed by the customer. Morgan Stanley has begun blocking them, JPMorgan, Bank of America and Citi are restricting them, and most banks are moving toward read-only access. For web developers the point is plain API and auth work: read-only by default, a separate revocable key instead of a password, and a log of who asked for what. Different from earlier packs (permissions in general, hosting cost, swappable models, search cost, long jobs, monitoring, repeatable evals): today is about read-only first and never sharing a person's password with an agent.

Sources: [SecurityWeek, In Other News, Oct 23](https://www.securityweek.com/in-other-news-tor-exit-relays-hijacked-banks-spot-rogue-ai-agents-self-rebuilding-macos-ransomware/).
