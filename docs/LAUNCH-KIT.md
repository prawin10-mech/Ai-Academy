# Launch kit

Replace https://ai-academy-mauve-five.vercel.app with your address (for example https://ai-academy-mauve-five.vercel.app). Read each community's self-promotion rules before posting, and do not post the same text everywhere on the same day.

## Before you post (10 minutes)
1. In Vercel, open the project, then Analytics, and press Enable. The site already loads the counter; it starts counting once enabled.
2. Set `APP_URL` in Vercel to your final address and redeploy. Share previews and the sitemap use it.
3. Paste https://ai-academy-mauve-five.vercel.app into a WhatsApp chat to yourself and check that the preview card shows. Use https://www.opengraph.xyz to check LinkedIn and X.
4. Open https://ai-academy-mauve-five.vercel.app/ai-engineer-roadmap while signed out. This is the page to share and the one search engines can read.
5. Submit https://ai-academy-mauve-five.vercel.app to Google Search Console and add the sitemap https://ai-academy-mauve-five.vercel.app/sitemap.xml.
